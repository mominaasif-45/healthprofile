# Emergency contacts

*Fictional policy for the HealthProfile demo. Synthetic example content, not real clinic rules and not medical advice.*

A member can add up to three emergency contacts to a profile. Exactly one of them is the primary contact.

* The first contact a member adds becomes the primary contact.
* To change the primary contact, mark a different contact as primary. The current primary contact cannot simply be
  unmarked, because a profile with contacts must always have a primary contact.
* Each contact needs a name, a relationship (for example parent, sibling or friend) and a phone number.
* Phone numbers may use spaces, brackets or dashes, and must have between 7 and 15 digits. The same number cannot be
  added twice to one profile.

In the demo, all contacts are fictional people. Never enter a real person's details.
