# Profile sections

*Fictional policy for the HealthProfile demo. Synthetic example content, not real clinic rules and not medical advice.*

A HealthProfile demo profile has four sections:

1. **Demographics**: a full name, and a date of birth or an age range (one of the two is required). Preferred
   name, gender and city are optional.
2. **Emergency contacts**: at least one person to contact.
3. **Wellness preferences**: a contact preference (required), and optionally an activity level, a diet
   preference, a sleep goal of 4 to 12 hours, allergies and lifestyle notes.
4. **Wellness goals**: at least one goal, such as better sleep.

Each complete section adds 25% to the profile's completeness, so a profile with all four sections is 100% complete.
The percentage is worked out again every time a section is saved.

A member can view and edit only their own profile. If an admin deactivates a profile, the member can still sign in
but cannot change the profile until it is reactivated.
