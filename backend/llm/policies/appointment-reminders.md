# Appointment reminders

*Fictional policy for the HealthProfile demo. Synthetic example content, not real clinic rules and not medical advice.*

In the HealthProfile demo, the fictional clinic sends one reminder for each booked appointment, two days before it.

* Reminders are sent by the member's contact preference: email, phone call or text message.
* A member can change the contact preference in the wellness preferences section of the profile.
* When the no-show estimate is above the 22% line, the fictional clinic may send one extra reminder the day before.
  An extra reminder is a friendly nudge, not a judgement about the member.
* The demo does not really send messages. Reminders are described here only to give the FAQ assistant something to
  answer.
